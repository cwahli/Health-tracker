# A. Executive 1-on-1 Talking Points Guide

## Meeting Goal
De-escalate managerial anxiety, present objective contextual clarity, and pivot from retrospective blame to forward-looking, measurable milestones.

---

### Phase 1: Collaborative Opening (60 Seconds)
- "Thanks for taking the time to sync today. I've taken time to reflect on our review conversation."
- "My primary goal is to ensure we are 100% aligned on what success looks like for the team and for my contributions."
- "I want to share some additional technical context and data from this cycle, and then look ahead at how we partner to hit our Q targets."

### Phase 2: Factual Clarification (Calm & Evidence-Backed)
- **On Project Delivery**: "Regarding [Project Name], while the final deployment shifted by [X] days, that delay was driven by [upstream dependency/unplanned blocker]. We communicated this on [Date] in [Channel]. Here is the data on what was delivered..."
- **On Quality & Output**: "When looking at overall system stability, our changes reduced error rates by [X]% and shipped [Y] features with zero P0 incidents."

### Phase 3: Forward Alignment & Mutual Commitments
- "To make sure there is zero ambiguity going forward, I've outlined a concrete 30/60/90 day plan with clear, measurable deliverables."
- "Can we review these proposed milestones together today and agree on what 'exceeds expectations' looks like for each?"
- "Let's set a recurring 15-minute weekly checkpoint dedicated strictly to this milestone tracker."

### Phase 4: Trap Defense & Boundary Management
- *If manager says: "You shouldn't need this level of hand-holding."*
  - **Response**: "I completely agree. The purpose of this framework isn't hand-holding; it's calibration. Once we validate these milestones, I will run with full autonomy."
- *If manager presents a PIP document to sign:*
  - **Response**: "I appreciate you sharing this. I take my performance and our team's mission seriously. I would like 24 hours to review the document and provide my written acknowledgment and proposed milestone alignments."
